Acoustic Activity

Y axis: sound level in dB SPL (sound pressure level) - the standard scale, zeroed at 20 micropascals. Calibrated so 94 dB SPL equals 1 Pascal, the industry-standard test tone. Hover or tap a point for its exact time, level, and how long the sound lasted. On long windows the chart shows the loudest event per interval.

Air Quality (CO2)

Y axis: CO2 concentration in parts per million (ppm). Hover or tap a point for the exact time and every sensor reading behind it, plus why that record was sent. On long windows the chart shows the highest-CO2 record per interval.
